h3. Description
When a deposit transaction sitting in *init order* status is automatically rejected after 5 minutes, no push notification is sent to the client informing them of the rejection. The transaction's own status changes/auto-rejects as expected, but the client is never notified on their device.

*Environment:* Test Environment (QA)
*Module / Feature:* Notification Pushing (Notifications / Deposit / Backend API)
*QA Owner:* Si

h3. Steps to reproduce
# Initiate a deposit transaction so that it sits in *init order* status.
# Wait 5 minutes, until the system automatically rejects the transaction.
# Check the client's device for a push notification about the rejection.

h3. Expected result
Once the 5-minute timeout elapses and the deposit is auto-rejected, a push notification informing the client of the rejection should be sent to their device.

h3. Actual result
The transaction's status changes to rejected as expected, but no push notification is delivered to the client - they have no on-device indication that their deposit was rejected.

h3. Impact
A member whose deposit times out and auto-rejects gets no notification at all, unlike (presumably) a deposit rejected by an operator. They would only discover the rejection by checking their transaction history themselves, which is a silent failure from the client's point of view.
